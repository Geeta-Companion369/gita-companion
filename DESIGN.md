# Design Brief: Bhagavad Gita Companion — Sacred Digital Satyug — FINAL PUBLISH

## Visual Direction
Sacred illuminated manuscript at maximum luminosity — bold vibrant colors at full strength, never dull. Candlelit warmth, ornate illuminated borders, temple arch headers, wax-seal buttons, sacred geometry. Every screen feels like 500-year-old temple Gita by candlelight, impossible to put down. Kurukshetra blurred battlefield background consistently applied to all non-reading screens as sacred common backdrop.

## Tone & Concept
Deeply spiritual, reverent, luminous. Turning pages of illuminated manuscript by candlelight in sacred temple. Sanskrit honored above all. Krishna's divine presence on every screen. Every color radiates divine energy—gold glows, saffron burns, lapis blue speaks infinite. Motion smooth and sacred, never jarring.

**Mono:** GeistMono (references only).

## Shape Language
Border-radius: minimal (2–4px). Wax-seal buttons 4px. Pages aged, not modern. Candlelight glow expands edges. Left spine hard corner. Shadows create 3D depth.

## Spacing & Rhythm
2.5rem between verse sections. 1.5rem vertical within. 2rem horizontal padding. Generous margins like old books. Breathing room is meditation.

## Enhanced Luxuries (Final 20%)
- **Shadows:** 6+ layer depth with gold glow halos (24–64px spread), inset luminous highlights, 3D elevated effect
- **Borders:** Luxury gradient borders animate with 6s cycle, dual-layer shimmer (outer gold, inner sacred glow), jewel-card effect
- **Glows:** Divine glow with 48–80px spread, 2-layer color (gold + sacred violet), breathing animation 3s cycle
- **Particles:** Gold dust glitter rain 28+ particles, lotus petals floating 4s cycle, constellation sparkles
- **Textures:** Aged grain 0.06 opacity, aged parchment corners, right edge shadow, inset manuscript lighting
- **Backgrounds:** Kurukshetra blurred battlefield (non-reading), animated gradient tiles (10s cycle), rainbow cards 0.94–0.96 opacity for readability
